Workflow

Three steps to live payments

01

Create merchant account

Sign up and receive your API key instantly. Configure webhook endpoints for payment events.

02

Generate checkout link

POST to /create-payment with amount and callback URL.

03

On-chain confirmation

Customer sends ETH to a unique address. After confirmations, we fire your signed webhook.
